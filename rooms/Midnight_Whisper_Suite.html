Midnight Whisper Suite

Room Info

Step into the Midnight Whisper Suite and settle in for a relaxing evening in the mountains. Deep midnight-blue bedding, natural wood beams, warm lighting, and decor inspired by stars and mountain nights give the suite a cozy cabin feel. A king-size bed faces the stone fireplace and television, making it easy to relax and enjoy a quiet night in.

Glass doors open onto a private balcony with breathtaking views of the San Juan Mountains and a telescope for stargazing beneath the night sky. The suite also includes a love seat, coffee and tea station, stocked mini-fridge, soaking tub, and spacious rainfall shower.

Suite Amenities

  • King-size bed with plush midnight-blue bedding
  • Complimentary stargazing kit and star map
  • Large flat-screen television for cozy movie nights

  • Luxurious bathrobes and slippers for guest comfort
